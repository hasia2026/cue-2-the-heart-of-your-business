## First-touch emails (personalize {name}; send Tue/Thu mornings)

### Dealers (subject lines rotate)

**Subject: $4,951 your service lane earned but never billed**

> Hi {name} — we scanned a franchise store in Riverside using 30 days of their own RO export and found **$4,951 in completed repair orders that never got invoiced**. Techs did the work; the invoices just never went out. Oldest was 15 days.
>
> Same scan also caught 14 leads nobody called back — about **$460k in pipeline** going cold.
>
> I run the same 20-minute scan free on any store's numbers — you keep the data, I bring the tool. If it finds nothing, I'll tell you and leave you alone. Could I swing by {Dealership} this week, or should I send the scan link?

**Subject: Who calls your internet leads in the first 48 hours?**

> {name} — half of buyers call the next dealer within 48 hours, and the last store we scanned had 14 internet leads sitting untouched for 21 days (~$460k worth). Not an effort problem — a visibility problem: nothing flagged the misses.
>
> I built a scanner that reads your own lead log and RO export and shows every leak in dollars, each one traced to a real record. Free 20-minute scan; if the scan is wrong, you lose a coffee break. Using {Dealership}'s numbers, this week — yes?

**Warm walk-in follow-up (same-day, after the visit)**

> {name} — good meeting you at {Dealership} today. As promised: the scan takes 20 minutes on your last-30-days RO + lead export — nothing moves without your approval. The one-page pilot sheet I brought is attached; $0–199 one-time, and if the pilot doesn't surface ≥$1,000 of documented leakage, it costs nothing. Want to start Monday?
