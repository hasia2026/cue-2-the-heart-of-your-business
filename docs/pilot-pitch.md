## The problem

Small businesses — car dealerships, HVAC shops, dental practices — lose 1–3% of revenue
in boring, invisible ways. Not fraud. Not bad strategy. **Drop-throughs:**

- Work that got finished but was never billed. The tech did the job; the invoice never went out. Customers don't chase bills that don't exist.
- Leads that called once and never got a call back. Half of buyers call the next store within 48 hours.
- Quotes that sat past their reprice window — the store's own policy already allowed the price move, but nobody pulled the trigger.

Nobody is lazy. This is a **visibility problem, not an effort problem**. On a $5M store,
that's **$50k–$150k a year** slipping away without a single alarm going off.

## What CUE is

You import data you already have — repair orders, leads, invoices, exports from the tools
you already pay for. CUE reads it and shows you, **in dollars, where money is leaking.**

> **"Find the money you're already earning but not collecting."**

Every number traces to a real record you can open. Nothing estimated, nothing invented.
It reads like a profit-and-loss conversation, not an AI demo.

A real scan of a Riverside dealership found: **$4,951** in unbilled repair orders,
**$460,000** in leads with no follow-up, **$66,000** of aging inventory past its
repricing window — about **$530,000 in play**, with **$31k recovered in 30 days**.

## The loop (five rooms, one building)

1. **Capture** — your messy records, in: file uploads, 30-second voice notes (auto-transcribed), document photos. English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Arabic.
2. **Understand** — deterministic detectors run your rules against your actual records and flag every miss with the evidence attached and a suggested fix, priced.
3. **Enhance** — strategy: what a repricing or re-engagement is worth *before* you commit (Compass, Pricing Studio).
4. **Execute** — **AI proposes. You decide.** CUE drafts the action and waits for your approval. Every step is logged in an audit trail you can hand to a bank or a partner.
5. **Expand** — referral campaigns, partner portals, scheduled digests (email + WhatsApp) with what CUE found each week.

The same engine speaks your industry's language — dealership vocabulary, HVAC vocabulary —
sitting **above** your existing tools, never replacing them.

## Why now / why this is different

Field-service AI answers phones and dispatches trucks — the *front door*. CRMs *store*
what someone typed. Your DMS holds the data and charges per seat for zero insight.
Nobody watches what happens **after** the truck leaves.

CUE combines three things none of them do: the full loop, approval-gated AI with a
real audit trail, and honest grounding — real numbers or no answer, never confabulated.
