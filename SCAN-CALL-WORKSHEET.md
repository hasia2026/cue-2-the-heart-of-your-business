# Scan-Call Worksheet — 20 Minutes to a Signed Pilot

*Print this (or keep it open) for every scan call. The structure: their numbers, live, in 15 minutes — then one ask. If CUE finds nothing, say so honestly and leave. The honesty *is* the pitch.*

---

## Before the call (5 min prep)

- [ ] Received their CSV export? **ROs/jobs + invoices + leads, last 30 days** (any format — even a messy spreadsheet works; CUE's detectors are column-tolerant).
- [ ] Know their revenue class ($2–5M single store? $10M+ multi-dept?) — sets the leak baseline (1–3% of revenue).
- [ ] /score result already emailed to them — have it open.
- [ ] Demo org (Northgate or Summit Air) loaded in another tab for the fallback if their export is late.

## The call

### 0:00–2:00 — Frame (their words, not yours)
> "You're going to drive; I'll type. We'll run your numbers through CUE's detectors live and you tell me if the findings are real. If nothing's there, I'll say so and you never hear from me again."

### 2:00–12:00 — Run the three detectors on THEIR export

**1. Unbilled completed work** *(the flagship — 60% of pilots convert on this alone)*
- Filter: completed/jobs-closed, invoice number empty or invoiced date > 24h after completion.
- Ask out loud: *"Did [technician name] actually do this work on [date]?"* — make THEM verify against their memory/system. **The owner verifying the leak is the sale.**

**2. Dead follow-up**
- Leads/estimates created 14+ days ago, no contact logged.
- Ask: *"Who owns follow-up when [advisor] is on a call?"* The gap IS the finding.

**3. Aging quotes/inventory**
- Quotes open 30+ days, or units/stock sitting past your repricing window.
- Ask: *"Your own policy allows [$X] repricing past 30 days — did anyone pull that trigger?"*

### 12:00–16:00 — The mirror moment
Put CUE on screen (demo org, evidence drawer open) and show the same detection running on data they can trace. Say:
> "Everything you just verified by memory, CUE caught from records — with the evidence attached, every time, without depending on anyone's memory or mood."

Key demo beats (pick what maps to their leaks):
- Insight card → evidence drawer (the SOP vs. the violation)
- Approve → workflow → task list ("CUE prepares, you decide")
- Audit trail ("every action is logged — you'll never wonder what the AI did")

### 16:00–20:00 — One ask, then sign

> "Here's my offer: 30 days, this one location, read-only export. If CUE doesn't identify at least $1,000 in documented leakage, you pay nothing and keep every finding. If it does, you decide whether [$299–799]/month is worth it. I'll set you up tonight, you look Thursday. Do we have a deal?"

- [ ] **Signed** → open [PILOT-AGREEMENT.md](PILOT-AGREEMENT.md), fill the brackets, agree the success metric (Section 3) out loud. Send it before you hang up.
- [ ] **"Send me something"** → they're not a no, they're a not-now. Book a 15-min follow-up *on the call* + queue the 3-touch follow-up (day 2: artifact screenshot; day 5: case-study post; day 9: honest breakup email).
- [ ] **"Found nothing"** → congratulations: you just earned trust. Ask for the one referral who complains about billing; log the call as honest-negative (it's a great story later).

## After the call (same day, 10 min)

- [ ] Log findings + objections in their CUE workspace notes (leak classes they confirmed).
- [ ] If signed: send pilot-agreement PDF + data-export checklist.
- [ ] Set the day-30 review with the metric in the calendar **now**.
- [ ] Audit trail note: every pilot conversion starts from a call where the customer verified a number — record which one. That's your first marketing artifact.
