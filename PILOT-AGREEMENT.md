**Between:** HASI Technologies ("CUE") and **[Company Legal Name]** ("Customer")
**Location / Store covered:** [single location address] · **Term:** 30 days from [start date]

## 1. What CUE does during the pilot

CUE connects to a **read-only export** of Customer's records [DMS / field-service platform / QuickBooks export — specify]. CUE analyzes the data, presents findings with evidence, drafts recommendations, and — only where Customer approves — generates task lists. **CUE prepares. Customer decides.** No recommendation executes without Customer's explicit approval.

## 2. Pay-for-proof

Pilot fee: **$0**. CUE earns the paid engagement only by finding real money:

> **Success threshold: CUE identifies at least $1,000 in documented revenue leakage** (completed-but-unbilled work, uncollected receivables, or lapsed follow-up), each item backed by a Customer-verifiable record.

- If the threshold is met, Customer may continue to a paid subscription at **[$299–799]/month** (by location, by modules used).
- If the threshold is **not** met, CUE says so in writing, hands over all findings free of charge, and the pilot ends. No invoice. No obligation.
- Customer may terminate the pilot at any time with one email.

## 3. Success metric (agreed before launch)

Primary metric: **[e.g., "completed work orders invoiced within 24h: 7 open → 0 within 30 days"]**
Measured from: [system of record]. Reviewed on [date] at pilot close.

## 4. Data handling

- Customer grants access to a **read-only export**; CUE never writes to Customer's systems of record.
- Data is stored in Customer's dedicated workspace, encrypted in transit and at rest, and is **deleted within 10 business days of pilot close** at Customer's request.
- CUE staff access Customer data only to operate the pilot; no data is used to train models or shared across customers.
- Customer owns its data at all times.

## 5. What CUE does not claim

Findings are computed from Customer's records and shown with evidence — they are estimates of recoverable value, not an audit. CUE does not guarantee collection amounts; it guarantees that every number it shows can be traced to a real record Customer can verify.

## 6. Confidentality & non-solicitation (standard, mutual)

Each party keeps the other's business information confidential for 2 years. Neither party poaches the other's staff during the pilot + 6 months.
