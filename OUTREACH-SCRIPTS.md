## C. The Scan-Call Script (20 minutes)

**Min 0–3 — Frame it:**
> "You keep your data; I bring the scanner. I'm going to ask for two exports — last month's closed ROs and your lead log — and run the same checks a dealership accountant wishes they had time for. Everything stays yours."

**Min 3–12 — Run CUE live on their numbers.** Silence is fine. Let the screen talk.

**Min 12–17 — Walk the findings:** "X unbilled ROs — that's $Y. Z leads cold. Which of these surprises you?" (The surprise is the sale.)

**Min 17–20 — The close:**
> "That's $X/month leaking. The pilot's job is to stop it — 30 days, one store, one metric we agree on today. If the scan is wrong about any of these, tell me and I'll fix the detector before we start. Want to begin Monday?"

**Objection handling:**
- *"We have a CRM."* → "Great — CUE sits above it and audits it. Your CRM stores leads; CUE catches the ones nobody touched."
- *"My team won't use another tool."* → "They don't have to. Week one, CUE just watches and emails you what it found. Adoption follows results."
- *"Is my data safe?"* → "Org-isolated database, encrypted connection, your exports stay yours, delete on request. Pilot NDA available."
- *"How much?"* → "Pilot is $0–199 one-time. If we find nothing, you owe nothing and I'll say so."

## D. Weekly cadence (one person, part-time)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | 10 LinkedIn DMs (A1/A2) |
| Tue | 1 group post (B1 or B3), 5 comments (B2) |

**Stop-doing rule:** if a channel produces zero scan bookings in 2 weeks, drop it for the other one. The funnel only needs ~5 scans and 2 pilots a month to hit 10 pilots in a quarter.
